I received my M.D. training at Zhejiang University School of Medicine and am expected to earn my doctoral degree in June 2026. My research has focused on understanding the molecular mechanisms underlying organ injury and regeneration. As part of my doctoral training, I also conducted research at Dana-Farber Cancer Institute, where I studied mitochondrial biology and metabolic regulation.

In the Zhou Laboratory, my current research interests lie in understanding the biological alterations underlying neurodegenerative disorders and identifying clinically relevant biomarkers that may improve disease prediction and patient stratification.
